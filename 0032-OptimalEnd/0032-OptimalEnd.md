# 0032 - Optimal End

- Status: Proposed
- Authors: Optim Labs

## Context

With the need to satisfy the liabilities of the OADA users we find it the right time to also liquidate the ODAO and satisfy any and all loose ends in the process. 
We propose a launch of a redemption portal for the O, OADA, and sOADA, as well as the legacy OPTIMiz assets, the owners of which will be able to redeem their holdings against by signing a claim message. The funds will be sent at the end of the claiming process, which is to take 10 weeks from the start of the launch, aimed at the 6th of October 2026 and ending 15th of December 2026. To launch at the discretion of Optim Labs, any delays will also move back the end of claiming. 
In order to claim, any and all claims on Optim Labs or Optim DAO must be renounced, and any claim on Splash Labs and Splash DAO given to Optim Labs to either pursue further reconciliation if deemed necessary. Unvested tokens are released per prior obligations outlined in Optim tokenomics documentation upon token launch. 

## Proposal

### Claims
We set the claim rate at
- 1 ADA per OADA
- 1 ADA per sOADA
- 0.3 ADA per O
- 0.01 ADA per OPTIMiz

Failure to sign a claim message in 10 weeks forfeits the claim, as the entities will wind down operation afterwards, mitigating legal costs to DAO. There will be one fanout claim to everyone who participated at the very end of the process i.e all claims are collected then distributed at the end of the claims period. In order to claim, any and all claims on Optim Labs or Optim DAO must be renounced, and any claim on Splash Labs and Splash DAO given to Optim Labs to either pursue further reconciliation if deemed necessary. 

### Funding
All of ODAO's assets shall be used for the purpose of funding claims, and any leftovers will be claimed by Optim Labs to make it whole on outstanding liabilities and operating costs. 
If fully claimed the ADA liabilities across all categories would be equal to approximately 4,600,000 ADA. 

### Authority to use 0030
We authorize the use of the capacity introduced in 0030 to withdraw the remaining funds from the protocol and use in the redemption period. 

### Winddown of Optim DAO
Optim DAO, ODAO shall wind down and cease operation at the end of the claiming process.
