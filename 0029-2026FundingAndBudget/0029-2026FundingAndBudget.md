# 0029-2026FundingAndBudget

- Status: Proposed
- Authors: Optim Labs

## Context

As Optim prepares for the 2026 ramp-up, prevailing ADA market conditions and obligations to Optim Labs and its partners—some denominated in ADA and O tokens—underscore the need for stable fiat funding to continue building through bear-market phases. Outstanding Catalyst milestones are progressing alongside OADA V3 and the work required to keep the protocol compatible with forthcoming Cardano hard-forks and future Layer-2 integration. Development of the Leviathian Layer 2 has resumed to enable more advanced Cardao Defi if market conditions improve, as comparable external initiatives from Cardano suited to have yet to materialize. To safeguard liquidity, credibility, and consistent progress, the DAO will evaluate future governance and legal options while providing fiat resources for development, infrastructure, and legal operations.
